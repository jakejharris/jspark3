## Derivation from the measured recipe

The public recipe is the measured recipe under the public identity. The
derivation changed file names and string literals only; no control flow,
contract hash, transform seam, serving argument, or environment value
changed. Because two overlay files carry identifiers, their hashes and the
loader hook's after-hash were re-pinned and proven: the derived patcher was
run against a pristine copy of the loader to produce the pinned after-hash,
and the measured patcher's output, mapped through the same renames, is

## Evidence policy

### Comparison taxonomy

Three evidence classes, labelled everywhere they appear and never merged into
one table, one delta, or one ranking:

| Class | Contents | Rule enforced by the validator |
|---|---|---|
| `Published reference recipes` | FlyCockpit TP3 `9093765c`, Mia TP2 `c190db1a`, jetnet TP3 `bfc820ec` and `4fdba004` plus the author's forum post, each with the fifteen minimum fields and an immutable source URL | Author-reported only; no percentage may share a table row or sentence with one of these values unless the percentage is itself author-reported |
| `Local reproduction of a published recipe` | `mia-tp2-historical-0e2e78f` (site/safety-adapted), `mia-tp2-current-c190db1a-adapted` (compatibility-adapted), `fly-derived-9093765c-adapted` (minimal-correctness/safety-adapted), and the exact current-Mia attempt that produced no number | Fidelity qualifier and pinned source commit required on every lineage; the failed attempt must carry no benchmark number; the adapted rapid screen stays marked rate-claim ineligible |
| `Internal ablation` | The matched three-Spark control (same recipe, overlay disabled), an unreleased internal development build: the single-stream pairing, pacing, scheduler, prefill, and the two missed gates | The denominator must be named as an unreleased internal development build; the bare word for it is refused anywhere in public prose |

The public comparison in `README.md`, `huggingface/README.md`,
`release/RELEASE-NOTES.md`, `release/ANNOUNCEMENT-BLOG.md`, and
`release/ANNOUNCEMENT-SOCIAL.md` is the published-reference class. The
internal ablation appears in `docs/BENCHMARKS.md` and
`docs/TECHNICAL-REPORT.md` under a heading that names it as unreleased
internal development evidence, and nowhere in a hero or headline.

### What is in the tree

Included: the three candidate single-stream batteries with per-request
results, windows, raw token streams, and sanitized counter deltas; the
earlier internal-ablation control battery; the three same-day matched
controls; the sanitized receipt sets for the three local reproductions of
published recipes and the author-reported reference table; the pacing
analyses; the matched scheduler and prefill receipts for both arms with their
checksum anchors; the agent demonstration receipts, prompt, counter monitor,
artifact, and screenshots; the matched control's demonstration summary; the
overlay census and admission-safety receipts; the campaign summary. Every
included file passed the builder's leak check and the validator's privacy
scan.

Excluded by policy: packet captures, pane and session logs, authorization
material, serving-manifest mounts, offline diagnostic container records,
host names, management and fabric addresses, container identities, run-root
paths, and any file over the size cap. The raw scheduler and prefill streams
are excluded; their summaries and checksum anchors are included.

Renamed on the public surface: the measured build is `JSpark3 v1`; the
internal configuration it was ablated against is "the matched three-Spark
control (same recipe, overlay disabled)", an unreleased internal development
build; the three local reproductions of published recipes carry the lineage
names `mia-tp2-historical-0e2e78f`, `mia-tp2-current-c190db1a-adapted`, and
`fly-derived-9093765c-adapted`; hosts are `rank0`, `rank1`, `rank2`.
